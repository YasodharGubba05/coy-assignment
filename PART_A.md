# Part A: two core features Coy should have

## 1. Anonymous, mutual-only icebreaker (a "first message" after a match)
**What:** When two people match, give them a lightweight in-app first step: a pick-one prompt ("Coffee or chai?"), a shared-interest question, or a time-limited chat (e.g. 48 hours) so the match turns into a conversation without leaving Coy.

**Why:** A match is the moment of highest emotion and also the moment of highest drop-off. Today the user gets a result and then has to work out what to do with it, usually by moving to Instagram or WhatsApp, which means Coy loses the engagement and the user loses the safety of the privacy promise. A guided first step increases match-to-conversation conversion, gives users a reason to return, and keeps the interaction inside the product that made it safe.

## 2. "Someone added you" anonymous signal, with a hint-level reveal
**What:** If someone has added you as a crush, show an anonymous indicator ("1 person is into you") without revealing who. Optionally let the user spend an invite or a slot to receive a coarse hint (same college or city), never a name. Identity is revealed only when the user adds that person too.

**Why:** Coy's value is the promise that interest is revealed only when mutual. The weak point is the user who has no idea anyone is interested and so never opens the app, and the person who added them never gets a result. A privacy-safe signal gives the non-user a concrete reason to sign up, add a crush and share the invite link, which feeds the viral loop. Interest stays private, and it creates a natural, honest reason to buy extra slots.
